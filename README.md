### The Philosophy: AI Writes Syntax. Humans Design Systems.

Anyone with an LLM prompt can generate a generic CRUD app in five minutes. That's not interesting. 

What AI *cannot* do is look at a broken, complex problem from an unexpected angle, connect two unrelated domains, and architect a system that shouldn't theoretically work—until it does. 

I don't obsess over memorizing boilerplate; I use AI and modern tooling relentlessly to compress execution time so I can focus 100% on **creative system design, unconventional problem-solving, and deep architecture** (mostly in **Rust**, **Python**, and **TypeScript**). I bring the blueprint, the constraints, and the lateral thinking; the code is just the final medium.

### Why Creative Architecture Matters (Selected Work)

Instead of building another wrapper, I like tackling problems that require rethinking the mechanics from the ground up:

* **[TypesetOK](https://github.com/Amlaach/typesetok) — Rethinking Desktop Publishing from Scratch**  
  *The Creative Leap:* Most developers avoid text shaping and layout engines because they are notoriously painful—especially when combining multi-stream flows with complex Right-to-Left (RTL) scripts and Hebrew vocalization (*nikkud*). Designing a reproducible PDF and typesetting engine in Rust required treating typography not as UI, but as a strict mathematical constraint problem.

* **[HDDE Architecture](https://github.com/Amlaach/HDDE-A-Zero-Allocation-Hierarchical-Utility-Architecture) — Zero-Allocation Decision Engine** `Research`  
  *The Creative Leap:* How do you run thousands of autonomous agents with deterministic predictability and zero memory allocations during runtime? By fusing Utility AI, hierarchical command propagation, and cache-coherent ECS into a single pipeline. A pure exercise in lateral performance thinking.

* **[Otzaria Semantic Search](https://github.com/Amlaach/otzaria-semantic-search) — Deep Context over Ancient & Complex Corpora**  
  *The Creative Leap:* Standard keyword search fails when dealing with dense, multi-layered textual corpora where meaning is implicit rather than explicit. This engine bridges modern high-density vector embeddings with fast Rust-based retrieval to find *ideas*, not just matching strings.
